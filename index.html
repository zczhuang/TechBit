About Meenal

A Neighbor Who Chose Belmont

I chose to make Belmont my home and have worked intentionally to lay down roots and become an active part of this community.

As a parent of two children in the Belmont Public Schools, I've been deeply engaged in our schools and town. I've served in several elementary school PTA leadership roles—including Secretary, Vice President, and Co-President—served as the parent representative on the Winn Brook principal search committee, participated in the CUE Report Card working group, and volunteered to campaign for our new library.

Professionally, I am a management consultant who helps organizations drive strategic growth and transformation. My work relies on data and analysis to evaluate options and guide decisions while working across diverse teams to build alignment and deliver practical solutions.

The experience of weighing tradeoffs, grounding decisions in evidence, and bringing different perspectives together is what I hope to bring to Town Meeting.

What I Stand For

My Priorities for Belmont

If elected, I would bring data-driven analysis and collaborative problem-solving to the issues that matter most to Precinct 8 and all of Belmont.

01

Top-Quality Education

Maintaining excellence across Belmont Public Schools—ensuring every student has access to the resources, teachers, and opportunities they deserve. Our schools are the cornerstone of our community.

  • Adequate and equitable school funding
  • Strong support for students and educators
  • Informed, transparent school governance
02

Financial Sustainability

Strengthening Belmont's long-term financial health through responsible cost management and diversified revenue sources—so we can deliver the services our community needs without undue burden on residents.

  • Evidence-based budget decisions
  • Diversified, sustainable revenue streams
  • Responsible stewardship of public funds
03

Civic Engagement

Encouraging broader participation in local democracy across all of Belmont's communities. A vibrant Town Meeting reflects the full richness of the people it represents.

  • Inclusive outreach across all neighborhoods
  • Transparent, accessible town governance
  • Bridging diverse community perspectives
Our Belmont

Why Belmont Matters

Belmont is more than a place to live—it's a community built on shared values: excellent schools, engaged neighbors, tree-lined streets, and a belief that where we live should reflect who we are.

I believe the decisions made at Town Meeting shape the quality of life for every resident of Precinct 8 and beyond. That's why I'm running—to bring a thoughtful, data-driven voice to these decisions, and to ensure Precinct 8 has an engaged, prepared representative at the table.

I would be honored to have your vote on Election Day, and I would be grateful for your help spreading the word—whether by mentioning my candidacy to neighbors, forwarding information, or connecting me with others who want to learn more.
